# Orthanc Analytics: brand kit

The reference for anyone writing, designing or updating the website (and, later, decks and product UI). If something on the site contradicts this file, one of the two is wrong: fix it.

## 1. Mission, positioning, audience

**Mission (draft).** Give Europe's political decision-makers evidence they can verify.

**Positioning.** Political intelligence for Europe, built on verifiable data. Live in Italy, built for Europe. For right-wing parties, campaigns and political groups across Europe.

**Core line.** *We analyse places, not people.* / *Analizziamo territori, non persone.*
Territorial intelligence from public aggregate data, with no voter files and no individual profiles. This is both an ethical position and the product's fit with EU rules (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2024/900 on political advertising).

**Audiences.**

| Primary | What they need from us |
|---|---|
| Party headquarters (strategy and data offices) | One verified view of the territory; numbers they can cite internally |
| Campaign managers | Where to send candidates, volunteers and budget; what changed since last time |
| Elected officials and their staff | Their territory between elections |
| Political foundations, think tanks | Historical results they can cite |

| Secondary | What they need from us |
|---|---|
| Investors | Problem, solution, why now, traction with context, team, data room |
| Press | Who we are, what we do and don't do |

## 2. Voice

Calm, precise, evidence-first. The site should feel "calm, clear, almost inevitable".

**Rules**

1. **Short sentences, plain words.** Explain a technical term the first time it appears ("uniform swing: the national change applied evenly to every constituency").
2. **Every number shows its source and date.** No number without a caption.
3. **Say what is not live.** "Not yet live", "preview", "not in force", "projected" are features, not weaknesses.
4. **No hype.** Avoid "revolutionary", "unprecedented", "cutting-edge", "AI-powered" as a selling point, "#1", "most accurate".
5. **Descriptive links.** Never "Learn more" / "Scopri di più". Write where the link goes: "Explore the Orthanc Polis platform", "Read the investor overview".
6. **Always "Orthanc Polis"**, never "Polis" alone in headings or first mentions: "Orthanc" is also an open-source medical-imaging server and "Polis" (Pol.is) a deliberation tool.
7. **Italian: "tu"** for the reader, consistently. Typographic apostrophes (’) and Italian quotes («»).

**Words to avoid (legal and reputational risk)**

| Avoid | Use instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| microtargeting, voter targeting, profiling, persuasion, "influence voters" | territorial prioritisation, campaign planning, resource allocation | Reg. (EU) 2024/900 art. 18; AI Act Annex III treats AI meant to influence voting behaviour as high-risk |
| "no personal data", "anonymous" | "no voter-level personal data" | Accounts and emails are personal data |
| "GDPR-certified", "compliant", "exempt" | "designed with the GDPR in mind" | There is no GDPR certification; compliance is a legal assessment |
| "fact-checked AI" | "AI-drafted, checked claim by claim against the sources, labelled as AI synthesis" | Describe the mechanism, not a guarantee |
| sondaggi / polls (for our projections) | proiezioni / projections, scenari / scenarios | Projections are model outputs; L. 28/2000 regulates poll publication |
| dominate, battlefield, target (people), weapon | win, territory, priority | Calm tone; no militarised language |

**Examples**

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "Revolutionary AI that predicts elections with 99% accuracy." | "Projects all 147 single-member constituencies from the 2022 result onto current polls." |
| "Learn more →" | "Read how Orthanc Polis verifies its data →" |
| "Scopri di più →" | "Esplora tutti i moduli di Orthanc Polis →" |
| "We know every voter." | "We analyse places, not people." |

## 3. Logo

- **Mark.** Four-point X-shaped star with slightly concave spikes, an octagonal frame and a centre dot. Source: `src/assets/brand/orthanc-mark.svg` (uses `currentColor`; 200×200 view box). It is a redraw of the supplied lockup: replace it with the original vector when available, keeping the file name.
- **Lockup.** Mark + `ORTHANC` (Inter Tight 700, letter-spacing ≈ .38em, uppercase) above `ANALYTICS` (IBM Plex Mono, letter-spacing ≈ .42em, brand grey). Built in HTML (`lockup()` in `src/layout.mjs`) so it stays sharp and accessible.
- **Colour.** White on black (default) or black on white. Never gradients, glows or other colours.
- **Clear space.** At least the width of the centre octagon on every side.
- **Minimum size.** Mark 24 px on screen; favicon uses the mark on a black square (`favicon.svg`).
- **Raster versions.** `apple-touch-icon.png`, `orthanc-logo-512.png` and the social images are generated by `npm run images`.

## 4. Colour

Monochrome. Colour is information, so we almost never use it.

| Token | Dark (default) | Inverted section | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#000000` | `#ffffff` | Page background |
| `--bg-1` | `#0a0a0b` | `#f4f4f3` | Panels |
| `--fg` | `#ffffff` | `#000000` | Headings, key text, buttons |
| `--fg-2` | `#c9cdd4` | `#2b2f36` | Body text |
| `--muted` | `#8b93a1` | `#555b66` | Labels, captions (both ≥ 6.8:1 contrast) |
| `--line` | white 13% | black 14% | Hairlines |
| `--live` | `#4ade80` | n/a | **Only** "live" status dots |

Each page has one inverted (white) section at most, used for the most important argument (Method, Trust, Why now).

## 5. Typography

- **Inter Tight** (variable, self-hosted): headlines at weight 400 with tight tracking (−0.03em); body 16–17 px, line-height 1.6.
- **IBM Plex Mono** (self-hosted): uppercase labels, section indices (`[01] PLATFORM`), source captions, tags. Letter-spacing .12em.
- Fluid sizes with `clamp()` (tokens `--fs-1` to `--fs-4` in `styles.css`).
- Fonts are self-hosted, never loaded from Google: no third-party requests (privacy) and faster pages.

## 6. Layout and components

- Square corners, 1 px hairline grids, generous whitespace, a 12-column feel.
- Section pattern: mono index + label → large headline → one-sentence lead → content.
- Components: stat grid with sources, numbered expandable rows, tenet grid, cards, module tabs, pipeline, timeline, contact routes, legal prose with table of contents.
- Buttons: solid (primary) or outlined (secondary), always with a descriptive label and an arrow.
- Mobile first: design at 360–390 px first, then add columns at 600 / 960 / 1100 px.

## 7. Imagery

- **Dot-matrix maps** (Natural Earth data, generated by `npm run map`): Italy in white = live; the rest of Europe in grey.
- **Product visuals** in monochrome (`grayscale`, higher contrast). Label illustrative images as such.
- Schematics in the product page are abstract and marked "Schematic, not real data". Never show fabricated numbers as if they were real.
- No stock photos of crowds, flags, ballot boxes or politicians.

## 8. Motion and accessibility

- Motion is quiet: 8 px fade-up on scroll, a ticker with a pause button, hover arrows. Everything is disabled with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Target WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible focus, skip link, keyboard-operable tabs and menus, alt text, `lang` on every page, reflow at 320 px.

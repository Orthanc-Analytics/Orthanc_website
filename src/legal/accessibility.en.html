1. Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technologies.

2. The standard we follow

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, the standard referenced by the European norm EN 301 549 and by the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882), which Italy transposed with Legislative Decree 82/2022.

3. Conformance status

We believe this website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 AA: most of it meets the standard, and the known limitations are listed below. This is our own assessment, based on automated testing (axe-core) and manual checks of keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, contrast, zoom and mobile layout, carried out on {{updated}}.

4. What we have done

  • A semantic page structure with landmarks, one main heading per page and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Full keyboard access, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1, and text that can be enlarged to 200% without losing content.
  • Layouts that reflow on small screens without horizontal scrolling.
  • Text alternatives for images and maps; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technologies.
  • Animation kept to a minimum, and switched off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Every page declares its language, and the English and Italian versions link to each other.

5. Known limitations

  • Some product images (for example the Orthanc Solo and Orthanc Logos visuals) contain small text inside the image. The essential information is given in the surrounding text and in the image descriptions.
  • The buttons on the contact page open your email application. If none is set up on your device, use the email address shown on the page instead.
  • External pages linked from this website, such as the sources we cite, are outside our control and may not meet the same standard.

6. Feedback and contact

If you find a barrier on this website, or need information in a different format, write to {{email}} and tell us the page address and the problem you found. We aim to reply within 30 days.

7. Enforcement

If you are not satisfied with our reply, and where accessibility law applies to this website, you can contact AgID (Agenzia per l’Italia Digitale), the Italian authority responsible for digital accessibility: www.agid.gov.it.

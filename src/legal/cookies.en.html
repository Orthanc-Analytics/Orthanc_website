1. In short

This website does not use cookies. It also doesn’t use similar technologies that store or read information on your device, such as local storage, tracking pixels or fingerprinting. That’s why you don’t see a cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. Some are strictly necessary for a site to work; others are used for analytics, advertising or profiling. Under Article 122 of the Italian Personal Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003), which implements the EU ePrivacy Directive, only cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary may be used without your consent.

3. This website

  • No cookies, neither our own nor third-party ones.
  • No local storage. Your language choice is part of the address (English pages at the site root, Italian pages under /it/), so nothing needs to be saved on your device.
  • No analytics or advertising tools, and no social media plugins. Links to other websites are plain links: nothing is loaded from them until you click.
  • No third-party content. Fonts, images and scripts are served from our own hosting.

Our hosting provider, GitHub, keeps technical logs for security purposes, as described in the privacy policy. This doesn’t involve storing anything on your device.

4. Other websites

If you follow a link to another website, such as a news source or a social network, that website’s own cookie policy applies.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

You can delete and block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Because this site sets none, blocking them doesn’t change how it works.

6. Changes

If we ever add cookies or tools that need your consent, such as analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before using them, with “accept” and “reject” options given equal prominence, as the Italian data protection authority’s cookie guidelines of 10 June 2021 require.

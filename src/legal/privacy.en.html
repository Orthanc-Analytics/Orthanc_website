3. The data we process

3.1 Hosting and security logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit a page, your browser sends technical data that any web server receives, such as your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and the type of browser you use. GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security purposes. We do not receive these logs and we do not use them to identify you. GitHub processes this data under its own privacy statement.

3.2 Emails and demo requests

If you write to us, for example by using the buttons on our contact page, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write: typically your organisation, role, region and the reason for your request. We use this only to reply, to arrange a demo or meeting if you ask for one, and to keep a record of our correspondence.

3.3 What we don’t do

  • We don’t use cookies or similar technologies (see the cookie policy).
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don’t build visitor profiles.
  • We don’t load fonts, scripts or other content from third parties: everything on this site is served from our own hosting.
  • We don’t sell or rent personal data.

4. Legal bases

  • Hosting and security logs: our legitimate interest in keeping the website available and secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Replying to your messages and arranging demos or meetings: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Article 6(1)(b)) or, where no contract is in view, our legitimate interest in answering the enquiries we receive (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Keeping records when the law requires it, for example for tax or accounting purposes: compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

8. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability where it applies. Where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

To exercise your rights, write to {{privacyEmail}}. We will reply within one month, which the GDPR allows us to extend by two further months for complex requests.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it). You can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

9. Sensitive data

Information revealing political opinions is a special category of personal data under Article 9 of the GDPR. Please don’t send us this kind of information about yourself or other people, through this website or by email, unless it is strictly necessary. If you do send it, we will use it only to handle your request and will delete anything that isn’t needed.

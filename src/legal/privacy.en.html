3. The data we process

We receive personal data only when you write to us; our hosting provider also keeps technical logs. When you write, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. Each channel is described below with its purpose, legal basis and retention period.

3.1 Demo request

  • What we receive: the email you send with “Email a demo request” on the Contact page, typically with your organisation, country and region, role and next election.
  • Purpose: to reply, and to arrange and run the demo you asked for.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless a contract follows or the law requires us to keep the data longer.

3.2 Investor and data-room request

  • What we receive: the email you send with “Email investor relations” or “Request data-room access”, typically with your fund or organisation, role and investment focus.
  • Purpose: to reply, to send you the investor overview and to arrange access to the data room.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible investment (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in managing relations with prospective investors (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Retention: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless an agreement follows or the law requires us to keep the data longer.

3.3 Orthanc Logos waiting list

  • What we receive: the email you send with “Join the Orthanc Logos waiting list”.
  • Purpose: only to tell you when Orthanc Logos launches. We will not use your address for any other communication or marketing.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by asking to join the list (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 130 of the Italian Privacy Code, Legislative Decree 196/2003). You can withdraw it at any time by writing to {{email}}; this does not affect the lawfulness of what we did before.
  • Retention: until we announce the launch. We then delete the list. If you ask to leave it earlier, we delete your address straight away.

3.4 General enquiries

  • What we receive: the email you send with “Email the Orthanc team” or directly to {{email}}, for example about press or partnerships.
  • Purpose: to reply to your enquiry and keep a record of our correspondence.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering the messages we receive (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, where your enquiry is about a possible contract, steps taken at your request (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Retention: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep the data longer.

Where the law requires us to keep records, for example for tax or accounting purposes, we keep them for the period it sets (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

3.5 Hosting and security logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit a page, your browser sends technical data that any web server receives, such as your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and the type of browser you use. GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security purposes. We do not receive these logs and we do not use them to identify you. GitHub processes this data under its own privacy statement.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the website available and secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: for the period GitHub sets for security purposes. We do not keep copies.

  • We don’t load fonts, scripts or other content from third parties: everything on this site is served from our own hosting.

6. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability where it applies. Where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise your rights, write to {{email}}. We will reply within one month, which the GDPR allows us to extend by two further months for complex requests.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it). You can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

7. Sensitive data

Information revealing political opinions is a special category of personal data under Article 9 of the GDPR. Please don’t send us this kind of information about yourself or other people, through this website or by email, unless it is strictly necessary. If you do send it, we will use it only to handle your request and will delete anything that isn’t needed.
